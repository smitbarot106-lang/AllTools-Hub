About the Remove Duplicate Lines

This tool removes repeated lines from a list and keeps the first copy of each one, in the original order. Options let you ignore capital letters, ignore spaces at the start and end of a line, and drop empty lines.

Typical jobs are cleaning an email or keyword list, merging two lists into one, or tidying a log file. After cleaning, you can check how many lines are left with the Word Counter.

How to use it

  1. Paste your list with one item on each line.
  2. Choose your options. For example, tick "Ignore capital letters" to treat Apple and apple as the same.
  3. Click Remove duplicates.
  4. Copy the clean list or download it.

How it works

Each line is compared with the lines already kept. If an identical line has been seen before, it is skipped. With "Ignore capital letters" on, Apple and apple are treated as the same line.

Worked example

The list apple, banana, apple, cherry, banana (one per line) becomes apple, banana, cherry. Five lines were pasted, three were kept and two were removed.

Common uses

  • Cleaning a mailing or keyword list.
  • Merging two lists and removing the overlap.
  • Reducing a log file to its unique entries.
  • Checking how many distinct values a column contains.

Limitations

  • Only whole lines are compared. Two lines that differ by a single character are both kept.
  • The original order is kept, so the tool does not sort the list.
  • Lines that differ only in inner spacing (for example two spaces versus one) are treated as different.

Frequently asked questions

Which copy of a repeated line is kept?

The first one. Later copies are removed.

Does it sort my list?

No. The order of the lines you pasted is preserved.

How do I treat "Apple" and "apple" as the same?

Tick "Ignore capital letters" before you run the tool.

How many lines can I paste?

There is no fixed limit, but very large lists (hundreds of thousands of lines) may be slow in your browser.
