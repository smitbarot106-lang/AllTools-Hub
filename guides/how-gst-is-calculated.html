How GST is calculated

Last updated: 24 November 2026

What GST is

GST (Goods and Services Tax) is a tax added to the price of goods and services. Many countries use a similar tax under another name, such as VAT or sales tax. The calculation is the same: a percentage of the price is added to the price.

The rate depends on the country and on the type of product or service, and rates can change. Always check the current rate with the seller, a tax professional or the official tax authority.

Adding GST to a price

If the price does not include tax, multiply it by the rate and add the result.

GST amount = price x rate / 100
Total = price + GST amount

Example: a price of 2,500 with 18% GST. GST = 2,500 x 18 / 100 = 450, so the total is 2,950.

Removing GST from a total

If the total already includes GST, you cannot simply subtract the rate from the total. The tax was calculated on the price before tax, so you divide instead.

Price before GST = total / (1 + rate / 100)
GST amount = total - price before GST

Example: a total of 1,180 that includes 18% GST. Price before GST = 1,180 / 1.18 = 1,000, so the GST is 180. Subtracting 18% of 1,180 would give 967.60, which is wrong.

CGST and SGST

In India, GST on a sale within one state is split equally between the central government (CGST) and the state government (SGST). An 18% GST is therefore 9% CGST plus 9% SGST. For a sale between states, IGST is charged instead, at the full rate.

Common mistakes

  • Subtracting the percentage from a tax-inclusive total instead of dividing.
  • Using the wrong rate for the product.
  • Forgetting that a discount is applied before tax, so the tax is charged on the reduced price.
  • Rounding each step too early and ending up a few cents away from the invoice.

Frequently asked questions

Is GST added before or after a discount?

Normally the discount reduces the price first, and GST is charged on the discounted price. Check the invoice, because exceptions exist.

Can I use the same method for VAT?

Yes. Replace the GST rate with your VAT rate.
