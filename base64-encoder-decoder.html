Base64 Encoder and Decoder

Base64 is a way to write data using only letters, numbers and a few symbols. It is often used in web addresses, emails and APIs. This tool encodes text to Base64 or decodes Base64 back to text.

Please note: Base64 is not encryption. Anyone can decode it, so never use it to hide passwords.

How to use this tool

  1. Paste your text or your Base64 code.
  2. Choose Encode (text to Base64) or Decode (Base64 to text).
  3. Click Convert.
  4. Copy the result.

Your privacy

This tool works inside your browser. What you type is not uploaded, saved or shared.
