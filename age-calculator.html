About the Age Calculator

The age calculator finds an exact age in years, months and days from a date of birth. It also shows the total number of days lived and how long it is until the next birthday. You can check the age on today's date or on any other date.

Use it for forms that ask for an exact age, for planning a birthday, or for checking eligibility on a particular day. The same date arithmetic is handy for dates in the past or future, for example the age on a school admission cut-off date.

How to use it

  1. Choose the date of birth.
  2. Leave "Age on this date" empty to use today, or pick another date.
  3. Click Calculate age.

How it works

  • The calculator subtracts the date of birth from the chosen date, borrowing days from the previous month when needed, then borrowing months from the year.
  • Total days = the number of calendar days between the two dates. Days to the next birthday = the days until the next occurrence of the same month and day.

Worked example

Someone born on 15 May 1990 is 36 years, 6 months and 9 days old on 24 November 2026, and has lived 13,342 days.

Common uses

  • Filling in forms that need an exact age.
  • Checking age limits for an exam, job or competition on a specific date.
  • Finding how many days remain until a birthday.
  • Calculating the age a person will be on a future date.

Limitations

  • A date of birth on 29 February is treated as 28 February in non-leap years for the "months and days" step.
  • The calculation uses calendar dates only and ignores time of day and time zones.
  • Dates must be entered in the date picker's format for your browser.

Frequently asked questions

Can I find age on a past or future date?

Yes. Fill in "Age on this date" and the calculator will use it instead of today.

Does it handle leap years?

Yes. The number of days in each month is taken from the real calendar.

Why does the next-birthday result say "Today"?

Because the date you chose falls on the day and month of the birthday.

Is my date of birth stored?

No. The calculation happens in your browser and nothing is uploaded.
