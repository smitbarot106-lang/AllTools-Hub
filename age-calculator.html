Age Calculator

Enter a date of birth to see an exact age in years, months and days, the total days lived and how long until the next birthday. You can also check the age on any other date.

How to use this tool

  1. Choose the date of birth.
  2. Leave "Age on this date" empty to use today, or pick another date.
  3. Click Calculate age.

Your privacy

This tool works inside your browser. What you type is not uploaded, saved or shared.
