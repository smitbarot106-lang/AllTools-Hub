About the Length Converter

The length converter changes a distance or measurement from one unit to another: millimetres, centimetres, metres, kilometres, inches, feet, yards and miles. Enter a value, pick the units and you also see the same length in every other unit.

It is useful for travel distances, DIY projects, sports, school work and product sizes. Converting height for health calculations? The BMI Calculator accepts inches directly. For mass, use the Weight Converter.

How to use it

  1. Type the number you want to convert.
  2. Choose the unit you start with in "From".
  3. Choose the unit you want in "To".
  4. Click Convert. The result and the same value in all other units appear below.

How it works

  • Each unit is defined in metres, and conversions go through metres: result = value x (metres per from-unit) / (metres per to-unit).
  • 1 inch = 0.0254 m, 1 foot = 0.3048 m, 1 yard = 0.9144 m, 1 mile = 1,609.344 m. These are the exact international definitions.

Worked example

10 kilometres = 10 x 1,000 / 1,609.344 = 6.2137 miles.

Common uses

  • Converting road distances between kilometres and miles.
  • Switching between feet and metres for heights and room sizes.
  • Checking screen, furniture or luggage dimensions in another unit.

Limitations

  • Results are shown to 10 significant digits, which is more than most uses need.
  • Only common metric and imperial units are included. Nautical miles and astronomical units are not.

Frequently asked questions

How many miles are in a kilometre?

One kilometre is about 0.621371 miles.

How many centimetres are in an inch?

Exactly 2.54 centimetres.

Are the conversions exact?

The factors are the exact international definitions. Displayed numbers are rounded to 10 significant digits.

Can I enter decimals and negative numbers?

Yes. Any number is accepted.
