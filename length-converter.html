Length Converter

Convert a length from one unit to another, such as kilometres to miles or feet to centimetres.

How to use this tool

  1. Type the number you want to convert.
  2. Choose the unit you start with in "From".
  3. Choose the unit you want in "To".
  4. Click Convert. The result and the same value in all other units appear below.

Your privacy

This tool works inside your browser. What you type is not uploaded, saved or shared.
