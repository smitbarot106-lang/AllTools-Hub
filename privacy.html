Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

This page explains, in plain language, how AllTools Hub handles information when you visit our website and use our free online tools.

What you enter into our tools

AllTools Hub is designed with privacy by default. Every tool on this website runs directly inside your web browser using client-side JavaScript.

  • The text, numbers, calculations and settings you enter are processed locally in your device's memory.
  • We do not transmit, upload, log or store your tool inputs or calculation results on our servers or with any third party.
  • When you copy results to your clipboard or download a generated file, that action takes place entirely on your device.

Google AdSense and advertising cookies

We have integrated Google AdSense code on our website for account verification and site review.

  • Current status: AllTools Hub is currently under review by Google AdSense. Advertisements are not yet actively being served on the website.
  • Prospective ad serving and cookies: Once our website is approved and advertising begins, third-party vendors, including Google, will use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites on the internet.
  • Personalized advertising: Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to AllTools Hub and/or other sites across the internet.
  • Opt-out choices: You can opt out of personalized advertising at any time by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of third-party advertising cookies by visiting aboutads.info/choices or youronlinechoices.com.

Web analytics

Cloudflare Web Analytics client-side tracking (beacon script) is not currently active on this website. We do not use third-party analytics trackers, tracking pixels or session recording scripts.

Local storage on your device

We do not use cookies for site functionality. Our website uses your browser's local storage (localStorage) solely to remember your preferred theme (light or dark mode). This setting stays on your device, contains no personal information, and can be cleared at any time in your browser settings.

Hosting and security

This website is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. As a reverse proxy and content delivery network (CDN), Cloudflare processes standard technical network connection data (such as visitor IP addresses, browser user agent strings and requested URLs) to route traffic and protect the site against attacks. We do not maintain or store access server logs directly. For details, please see the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

External links

Our website links to external platforms such as GitHub. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of external websites.

Changes to this policy

If our advertising status changes or if we introduce new features, we will update this page and revise the date above.

Questions
