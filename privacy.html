Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

This page explains, in plain language, what happens to information when you use AllTools Hub.

What you enter into our tools

Our tools run inside your web browser. The text, numbers and settings you enter are processed on your own device. We do not upload them to our servers, and we cannot see them.

Information we do not collect

  • We do not ask you to create an account.
  • We do not ask for your name, email address or phone number to use a tool.
  • We do not set advertising or tracking cookies, and we do not currently use analytics or show ads.

What is stored on your device

If you choose light or dark mode, your choice is saved in your browser's local storage so the site can remember it. It stays on your device. You can clear it at any time in your browser settings.

Hosting

This website is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Like most web hosts, Cloudflare may process technical information such as your IP address and browser type to deliver the site and keep it secure. Please see Cloudflare's own privacy policy for details.

Links to other sites

Our contact page links to GitHub. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of other websites.

Changes to this policy

If we add features such as analytics or advertising, we will update this page before they start and change the date above.

Questions

If you have a question about privacy, please use our contact page.
