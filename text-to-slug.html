About the Text to Slug Converter

A slug is the readable last part of a web address, such as the part after the final slash in /blog/my-first-blog-post. This tool converts any title into a clean slug that uses only lowercase letters, numbers and dashes.

Short, descriptive slugs are easier to read, share and remember than long addresses full of codes. Accented letters are simplified (café becomes cafe) and punctuation is removed. To change the capitals in a title first, use the Case Converter.

How to use it

  1. Type or paste your title.
  2. Choose a dash (-) or an underscore (_) to separate words.
  3. Click Make slug.
  4. Copy the slug and use it in your web address.

How it works

1. Accents are removed. 2. Letters are made lowercase. 3. Every run of characters that is not a letter or number becomes one separator (a dash or an underscore). 4. Separators at the start and end are trimmed.

Worked example

The title "10 Tips for Better Sleep!" becomes 10-tips-for-better-sleep.

Common uses

  • Creating the address for a blog post or product page.
  • Naming files so they are safe to upload and link to.
  • Building consistent identifiers for a database or content system.

Limitations

  • Letters from non-Latin alphabets, such as Hindi, Arabic or Chinese, cannot be turned into slug letters and are left out. If nothing is left, the tool shows an error.
  • The tool does not check whether the slug already exists on your site.

Frequently asked questions

Should I use a dash or an underscore?

Dashes are the usual choice for web addresses because they separate words clearly. Underscores are common in file names and code.

Does the slug affect search rankings?

A clear slug helps people understand a link, but it is only one small signal. Do not expect a slug alone to change your ranking.

Why was part of my title removed?

Only English letters and numbers are kept. Other characters, symbols and non-Latin letters are dropped.

Can I change a slug after publishing?

You can, but the old address will stop working unless you redirect it to the new one.
