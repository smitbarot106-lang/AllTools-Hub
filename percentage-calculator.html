About the Percentage Calculator

The percentage calculator answers the three questions people ask most often: what is a percentage of a number, what percentage is one number of another, and by what percentage has a value changed.

Percentages appear in shopping, grades, interest, statistics and tax. If you are working with prices, the Discount Calculator and the GST Calculator apply the same ideas to money.

How to use it

  1. Choose the question you want answered.
  2. Enter number A and number B. The question you chose shows how each number is used.
  3. Click Calculate.

How it works

  • A% of B = A / 100 x B.
  • A is what percent of B = A / B x 100.
  • Percent change from A to B = (B - A) / |A| x 100. A positive result is an increase and a negative result is a decrease.

Worked examples

15% of 240 is 36. The number 45 is 25% of 180. A value that goes from 80 to 100 has increased by 25%.

Common uses

  • Working out a tip, commission or share of a total.
  • Finding what fraction of a budget or score a number represents.
  • Measuring how much a price, salary or measurement has grown or fallen.

Limitations

  • A percent change cannot be calculated from a starting value of zero, because the change would be infinite.
  • A rise of 25% followed by a fall of 25% does not return to the start, because each percentage applies to a different base.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate a percentage of a number?

Divide the percentage by 100 and multiply by the number. For 15% of 240: 15 / 100 x 240 = 36.

What is the difference between percent change and percentage points?

If a rate moves from 10% to 12%, it rises by 2 percentage points, which is a 20% increase in the rate.

Why does a 50% increase then a 50% decrease not cancel out?

The decrease is taken from the larger, increased number. 100 up 50% is 150, and 150 down 50% is 75.

Can I enter negative numbers?

Yes. Negative values are accepted for all three questions, apart from the zero cases described above.
