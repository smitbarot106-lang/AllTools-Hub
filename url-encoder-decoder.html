URL Encoder and Decoder

Web addresses can only contain certain characters. This tool replaces special characters, such as spaces and symbols, with safe codes like %20. It can also turn those codes back into normal text.

How to use this tool

  1. Paste your text or web address.
  2. Choose what you want to do.
  3. Click Convert.
  4. Copy the result.

Your privacy

This tool works inside your browser. What you type is not uploaded, saved or shared.
