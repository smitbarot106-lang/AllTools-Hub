Contact us

We would like to hear from you. You can contact us to report a bug, ask a question, or suggest a new tool.

How to reach us

Please open a new issue on our public project page: github.com/smitbarot106-lang/AllTools-Hub/issues. You will need a free GitHub account.

What to include

  • The name of the tool and the page address.
  • What you did, what you expected and what happened instead.
  • Your browser and device, if you know them.

Please do not post passwords, private documents or other personal information.
