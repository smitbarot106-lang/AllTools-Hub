About the Case Converter

The case converter changes the capital letters in your text in one step. Choose UPPERCASE, lowercase, Title Case or Sentence case for writing, or camelCase, PascalCase, snake_case and kebab-case for programming.

It is useful when text arrives in the wrong shape: a heading typed in capitals, a column of names in lowercase, or a phrase that has to become a variable name. If you need a web address instead of a variable, the Text to Slug Converter tool is a better fit.

How to use it

  1. Paste your text into the box.
  2. Choose the style you want.
  3. Click Convert.
  4. Click Copy to copy the new text, or Download to save it as a file.

How it works

  • Title Case capitalises the first letter of every word and lowercases the rest.
  • Sentence case capitalises the first letter after each full stop, question mark or exclamation mark.
  • camelCase, PascalCase, snake_case and kebab-case first split your text into words (letters and numbers), then join them in the chosen style.

Worked example

The text "hello world example" becomes helloWorldExample in camelCase, HelloWorldExample in PascalCase, hello_world_example in snake_case and hello-world-example in kebab-case.

Common uses

  • Fixing a heading or caption typed with caps lock on.
  • Turning a phrase into a variable or file name.
  • Making a list of names consistent before importing it.
  • Preparing text for a style guide that requires Title Case or Sentence case.

Limitations

  • Title Case capitalises every word. It does not skip small words such as "of" or "the", which some style guides leave in lowercase.
  • Programming styles keep only letters and numbers and drop punctuation.
  • Case rules for a few languages (for example Turkish dotted and dotless i) follow your browser's default rules.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between Title Case and Sentence case?

Title Case capitalises each word ("The Quick Brown Fox"). Sentence case capitalises only the first word of each sentence ("The quick brown fox").

What is camelCase used for?

Programmers use it for variable and function names, because names cannot contain spaces. The first word is lowercase and each later word starts with a capital letter.

Is my text sent anywhere?

No. The conversion runs in your browser.

Can I convert a large document?

Yes, but very large text can make the page slow on older phones. Convert it in sections if that happens.
