Case Converter

Change the capital letters in your text in one click. Choose UPPERCASE, lowercase, Title Case, Sentence case, or programming styles such as camelCase and snake_case.

How to use this tool

  1. Paste your text into the box.
  2. Choose the style you want.
  3. Click Convert.
  4. Click Copy to copy the new text, or Download to save it as a file.

Your privacy

This tool works inside your browser. What you type is not uploaded, saved or shared.
