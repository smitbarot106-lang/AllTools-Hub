About AllTools Hub

AllTools Hub is a collection of free online tools for everyday jobs: counting words, working out a loan, formatting data, converting units and more. Each tool is built to be clear for a first-time visitor and to show how its result is calculated.

How we work

  • Simple. Every tool explains what it does, what to enter and what you will get.
  • Private. Our tools run in your browser. The text and numbers you enter are not uploaded to our servers.
  • Transparent. Each tool page shows the formula behind the result, a worked example and the limits of the tool.
  • Free. You do not need an account to use any tool.

What is available today

There are currently 19 tools in 5 categories: text tools, calculators, developer tools, unit converters and other everyday tools. We also publish short guides that explain the maths behind the calculators. Every tool is covered by automated tests that check normal, empty, invalid and edge-case inputs.

How we make money

The site is free to use. We may show advertising from Google AdSense in the future, which would help pay for the work. Advertising never changes a tool's result. See the Privacy Policy for how advertising and cookies are handled.
